# MVP result report — 2026-10-01

All coordinates, RF observations, localization errors, packet delivery, animal
responses, and energy outputs below are **SIMULATED**. No physical tag, anchor,
farm, or cattle response was measured. The full raw benchmark is in `results/`;
the live demo uses a separate deterministic run.

## 1. Implemented

- Seeded 2D farm and herd movement, obstacles, log-distance/FSPL radio model,
  correlated shadowing, orientation/weather loss, NLOS, interference, noise,
  and packet loss.
- Virtual tag FSM/HAL and energy ledger; replaceable anchor/tag contracts;
  passive animal RFID, BLE/Wi-Fi, cellular, Sionna/ns-3 and Zephyr/Wokwi are
  modeled as interfaces/capability probes where appropriate.
- Six localization methods: strongest anchor, weighted centroid, path-loss
  inversion, Extra Trees, Gradient Boosting and temporal fusion. A GPS oracle is
  reported only as a separate zero-error reference after inference.
- FastAPI + SQLite local service, hash-chained animal events, experimental CSI
  and simulated virtual fence, Parquet/CSV train-validation-holdout export, and
  editable SVG dashboard with DEBUG-only truth and error lines.
- Reproducible benchmark tables, CDF plot, cost placeholders and this report.

## 2. Automated checks

`uv run pytest -q` passed **45 tests**. They cover RF propagation and loss,
movement, estimators, ML inference boundary, timestamp drift, tag state/energy,
identity/event hashes, dataset field separation, API privacy, CSI and virtual
fence. `git diff --check` also passed. TestClient emits a Starlette/httpx
deprecation warning; it does not fail the suite.

The live demo was started locally and checked at `/`, `/api/health`,
`/api/anchors`, `/api/positions`, `/api/metrics`, and `/api/positions?debug=true`.
For 100 animals/eight anchors it returned 100 estimates. The normal position
response had no ground-truth fields; DEBUG returned them.

## 3–6. Quantitative localization, energy and anchor tradeoff

The standard demo uses 100 animals, eight anchors, 1 km × 1 km, seed 7, 30 s
samples, and a 30 minute simulated episode. Its weighted-centroid results were:

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Mean error | 118.4 m |
| Median error | 103.9 m |
| P90 / P95 / P99 | 215.5 / 270.6 / 347.4 m |
| Maximum error | 534.7 m |
| Estimate coverage | 100% of simulated samples |
| Packet delivery ratio | 79.4% |
| Estimated energy | 0.156 mAh/tag/day |
| Messages | 101.2/tag/day |
| Battery life | Unavailable; no battery capacity is configured |

Coverage here means an estimator returned a position for each simulated sample;
it does not mean every point in the farm has RF coverage. Energy uses the
configured STM32WLE5 reference profile. Sleep/TX/RX inputs come from the brief;
other state currents are assumptions requiring board measurements.

In the benchmark's 100-animal scenario, weighted-centroid mean error changed
with anchor count as follows:

| Anchors | Mean error | P95 | Packet delivery |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 4 | 237.6 m | 547.7 m | 73.1% |
| 8 | 161.8 m | 349.0 m | 80.1% |
| 12 | 137.1 m | 352.9 m | 75.4% |
| 20 | 129.3 m | 269.2 m | 83.3% |

For this one simulated scenario, going from four to 20 anchors reduced mean
error by about 46%; the improvement from 12 to 20 was smaller. Across all 24
anchor-count × herd-size combinations, mean errors were 238.6 m (4 anchors),
145.4 m (8), 176.0 m (12), and 136.6 m (20). The non-monotonic 12-anchor result
reflects one random seed per combination and limited simulated samples; it is
not evidence that 12 anchors are intrinsically worse.

At 100 animals/eight anchors, the temporal filter averaged 154.3 m, slightly
below the 161.8 m weighted-centroid baseline. Extra Trees and Gradient Boosting
averaged 408.2 m and 419.1 m respectively. Fingerprint ML did not beat the
RSSI baseline despite randomized training conditions.

## 7. Robustness and limitations

The stress matrix used 100 animals, mixed behavior-driven movement, and 4/8/12/20
anchors. Weighted-centroid mean error averaged over those anchor counts:

| Scenario | Mean error | Packet delivery |
|---|---:|---:|
| LOS baseline | 148.3 m | 80.9% |
| 25% NLOS probability | 180.0 m | 76.2% |
| 50% configured packet loss | 291.0 m | 42.8% |
| One anchor disabled | 169.3 m | 84.2% of remaining anchor observations |
| One anchor corrupted by +40 dB | 389.3 m | 80.9% |
| Anchor clocks offset by ±250 ms | 148.3 m | 80.9% |

These averages are simulator-specific. Packet delivery under the 50% loss
setting also includes interference and receiver sensitivity effects. A broken
anchor can return plausible but wrong RSSI; the baseline estimator does not
automatically identify that fault. Timestamp clustering handled the tested
250 ms offsets; larger than 1 s offsets are not aligned.

Other material limits:

- There is no hardware-in-the-loop build. Zephyr `native_sim`, Wokwi, KiCad,
  ngspice, Sionna RT and ns-3 were not installed in this environment. The tag
  FSM is Python; capability probes do not imply those tools were run.
- The RFID reader is an interface only. Wi-Fi CSI is a toy stochastic trace
  marked EXPERIMENTAL, not PHY-derived CSI.
- The simulation constrains ordinary movement to the farm. Escape is an
  explicit scenario. No animal behavior classifier has been validated.
- Dataset holdout uses new random seeds/channel settings on the same nominal
  farm map and anchor layout. Transfer to a different farm layout is untested.
- Cost fields are blank and marked `PRICE_RESEARCH_REQUIRED`; no supplier
  quotes were researched. This MVP therefore cannot establish cheaper total
  cost than GNSS plus cellular.

## 8–9. Hypotheses

Not supported by this run: that a small RSSI fingerprint model automatically
outperforms a hand-built baseline; that four anchors provide fine-grained
localization; or that adding anchors produces a stable linear accuracy gain.
The GPS comparison is only an oracle, not a competing RF input. The run also
does not answer the commercial cost question.

Promising for a physical test: shared anchors delivered roughly 73–83% of
simulated packets in the 100-animal baseline; weighted/temporal estimates
improved as anchor count increased from four to 20 in that scenario; and bounded
clock skew did not break aggregation. Errors remain on the order of hundreds
of metres at the tail, so this is not yet useful livestock positioning.

## 10. Next physical experiment

Build four and eight receiver anchors around one surveyed paddock and equip ten
animals with prototype tags. Log raw receive timestamps/RSSI/SNR, packet loss,
IMU features and measured current while animals graze, rest, walk and pass behind
vegetation/buildings. Use an independent surveyed GNSS/RTK reference stream only
for evaluation, held out from inference. Compare the same weighted-centroid and
temporal estimators against this benchmark before increasing herd size. Measure
the complete board current profile and obtain dated component quotes at the
same time; those two measurements are required before claiming battery life or
cost advantage.
